Cognitive Control and Habit — Revealed Through Experimental, Multimodal Data

We are a Cognitive Neuroscience lab based in Madrid (Spain) studying how the brain controls behaviour and habits.
We combine neuroimaging, brain stimulation and computational approaches to understand cognitive control and
habit mechanisms in both healthy and clinical populations.
